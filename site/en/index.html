Quick start

Get started in 3 steps.

No config file, no terminal. In a minute you'll know how your server is doing.

  1. Download Pulo

    Download Pulo and drag the app into your Applications folder. You need macOS 27 or later.

  2. Add your server

    Enter your server's address, for example localhost:3000, and start the check.

  3. Keep an eye on your buddy

    Pulo shows you the status in plain language. If something goes wrong, you'll see it on his face.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Pulo?

Pulo is a macOS app that checks whether your local server is reachable and shows you the status in plain language. The buddy of the same name shows you with his face how your server is doing.

Is Pulo free?

Yes, there's a free version. Pro gives you extra features – see the comparison above for details.

Which macOS version do I need?

Pulo runs on macOS 27 or later.

Is Pulo available for Windows?

Not yet. A Windows version is at the top of the roadmap – join the waitlist there.

What about privacy?

Pulo checks your local servers directly from your Mac. You'll find all details about accounts and data in the privacy policy (German).

What data leaves my Mac?

Your server checks run locally on your Mac. Only when you buy Pro is the purchase handled through the App Store – Apple's privacy rules apply to that.
